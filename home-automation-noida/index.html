Smart homes in Noida

Why Noida homes choose Edge Smart Space.

Most Noida homes are flats in large housing societies, where wall chasing and drilling often need RWA permission. Our installation needs neither: concealed relays sit behind your existing switch plates, cameras mount without new conduits, and everything can be removed if you move.

Control lights, fans, ACs and the geyser from Google Home or Alexa, keep an eye on the balcony and front door, and pay nothing per month for cloud storage.

Renting? Take it with you

Because the installation is 100% reversible, tenants can automate a rented flat and take the system along when they move.

FAQs

Home automation in Noida: FAQs.

Will my society or RWA allow it?

Almost always — nothing is drilled or chased through walls, and the work stays inside your flat. If your society needs a work permit for any electrical work, we will help with the details.

Do you cover Greater Noida West?

Yes, including Noida Extension societies, as well as Greater Noida and the Noida Expressway sectors.

I live in a rented flat. Can I still automate it?

Yes. The installation is fully reversible, so we can restore the original setup when you move and reinstall the system in your next home.

What does it cost for a 2 BHK in Noida?

The 2 BHK Executive Suite is ₹44,999 + GST and includes 14 switch channels, a biometric door lock, AC/TV automation, a 360° camera and a Google Nest Hub.
